# BioExcel / EuroCC Workshop on Biomolecular Simulations: Aegean Edition

**18–20 November 2026 · Biomedical Research Foundation, Academy of Athens (BRFAA), Athens, Greece and online**

The sixth [BioExcel Ambassador Program](https://bioexcel.eu/about/governance/ambassador-program/) workshop, jointly organised and supported by the [National Competence Centre in Greece (EuroCC Greece)](https://eurocc-greece.gr/), the [Biomedical Research Foundation, Academy of Athens](https://www.bioacademy.gr/), the [Izmir Biomedicine and Genome Center, Dokuz Eylul University](https://ibg.edu.tr/?lang=en) and the [Hot4cryo project](https://hot4cryo.com/project/).

This hands-on, hybrid (on-site and online) workshop is aimed at PhD students and postdocs, and focuses on the BioExcel core codes **GROMACS**, **HADDOCK** and **BioBB**.

- Event page: [BioExcel / EuroCC workshop on Biomolecular Simulations: Aegean Edition](https://bioexcel.eu/events/bioexcel-eurocc-workshop-on-biomolecular-simulations-aegean-edition/)
- [GRNET workshop information](https://events.grnet.gr/event/211/)
- Venue: Biomedical Research Foundation, Academy of Athens (BRFAA), 4 Soranou Ephessiou, 115 27 Athens, Greece
